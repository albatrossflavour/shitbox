<completed_work>

- **Audio-before-video gap** (`ac4f6f6`): Cabin audio was starting at `cabin_shift` while PiP video only appeared at `pip_enable_t`. Fixed with `atrim=start=audio_trim` + `asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS` before `adelay`, so audio and video both appear at `pip_enable_t`.

- **setpts STARTPTS bug — 1.93s cabin offset** (`76755ab`): `setpts=PTS-STARTPTS+cabin_shift/TB` was wrong. When overlay `enable='gte(t,pip_enable_t)'` defers pulling cabin frames, STARTPTS is the first PTS seen by the filter (~1.932), not 0. Undershoots by that amount. Fixed by switching to `-itsoffset cabin_shift` before the cabin concat input — operates at demuxer level, unambiguous.

- **Brio 100 pipeline latency — 0.5s cabin behind** (`ead6912`): After itsoffset switch, residual 0.5s lag visible. Brio 100 V4L2 timestamps lead actual frame capture by ~0.5s (USB transfer + MJPEG encode pipeline). ELP H264 stream copy has near-zero latency. Fixed: `BRIO_PIPELINE_LATENCY_S = 0.5`, formula becomes `cabin_shift = head_offset_s + cabin_pts_offset - BRIO_PIPELINE_LATENCY_S`. Deployed via rsync to Pi.

- **Leading-silence audio confusion** (no code change): AAC stream has size-6 silence frames T=0 to T=10.97s, real audio from T=10.97s. Players skip to first substantive audio on first play; seek works correctly on scrub.
</completed_work>

<decisions_made>

- `-itsoffset` over `setpts` for cabin shift: `setpts STARTPTS` is unreliable when an overlay `enable` expression defers frame consumption. `-itsoffset` is a demuxer-level operation and is unambiguous.

- `BRIO_PIPELINE_LATENCY_S = 0.5`: Empirically derived. Brio 100 MJPEG pipeline adds ~0.5s between V4L2 timestamp and actual capture time. ELP stream copy has near-zero delay.

- `cabin_pts_offset` probed at render time: Brio at 15fps drifts segment boundaries relative to ELP at 30fps (~-1.93s after 6-8 mins, ~0.021s at startup). Probing actual first-packet PTS at render time corrects this drift automatically.
</decisions_made>
