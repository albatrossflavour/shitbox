<decisions_made>

- `setpts=PTS+cabin_shift/TB` in pip_chain (video only), not `-itsoffset`: `-itsoffset` is a demuxer-level operation that shifts all streams including audio. Since the audio atrim chain depends on `[1:a]` starting at raw PTS=0 from the concat demuxer, `-itsoffset` breaks it. `setpts` on `[1:v]` only is the correct tool.

- `BRIO_PIPELINE_LATENCY_S = 2.5`: Empirically derived. After the setpts fix revealed 2s residual cabin lag, the constant was stepped from 0.5 to 2.5. This corrects for the Brio 100 timestamp lead over actual capture time.
