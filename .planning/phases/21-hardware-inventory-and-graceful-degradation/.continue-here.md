## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)

- CSS flex + overflow trap on Pi 5 Chromium kiosk — `flex: 1` + `min-height: 0` + `overflow-y: auto` does not scroll reliably in full-screen modals at 1024×600. Use absolute pixel rails for the scroll container instead. Discovered after two failed fix attempts (`3439f08` and follow-up) before the absolute-rail rewrite in `fcf510f`.
- Strict DRM `status == "connected"` check on Pi 5 — EDID is cached after first hot-plug and subsequent reads report `unknown`. Treating `unknown` as missing false-flagged a working HDMI output for three days of operation. Accept anything except `disconnected`.
