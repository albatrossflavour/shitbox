## Key architectural decisions locked during planning

These came out of the checker iteration. Do not relitigate:

1. **`AlertStatus` shape is two sustain counters** (`active_sustain_count` + `clear_sustain_count`), not one. Pitfall 2 (sticky-bit recovery never fires) needs separate active/clear tracking.
2. **`recovery_subtype` kwarg lives inside 15-01's `fire_recovery` signature**, not as a 15-02 amendment. Keeps files_modified honest.
3. **SSE payload is exactly 5 scalar fields** (`role, label, tier, state, since_ms`). No `message`, no `last_seen`, no free-form `subtype` leakage.
4. **Frontend uses Alpine x-for**, not `document.getElementById` + `innerHTML`. Mirrors the existing hardware rendering pattern.
