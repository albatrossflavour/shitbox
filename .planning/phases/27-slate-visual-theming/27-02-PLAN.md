## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-27-02-01 | Denial of service | `_resolve_strings` hero transform on pathological geocoder output | mitigate | Operation order (`split` → `_truncate` → `.upper()`) preserves the existing `MAX_PLACE_CHARS=28` clamp before upper-casing.
