## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)

- **Reintroducing the upper-half alignment lip.** It moved to the tray for a reason — the upper's natural print orientation puts seam-down features at the build plate as fragile thin features.
- **Reintroducing `castle_gate_arch` or `castle_fan_arch_upper` without a print-orientation strategy.** Both were dropped specifically because they break the upper print. Revert only if you've solved the overhang.
- **Bundling unrelated uncommitted files into the next pi-case WIP commit.** `src/shitbox/events/engine.py`, `src/shitbox/sync/timelapse_compiler.py`, `.planning/STATE.md`, `.planning/ROADMAP.md` are leftovers from prior unrelated sessions.
- **Installing magnets without marking polarity first.** A mismatched corner repels and the lid won't seat. Trivial to prevent, painful to debug after gluing.
