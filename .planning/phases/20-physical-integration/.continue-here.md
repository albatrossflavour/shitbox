<decisions_made>

- OUTER_WALL 2.0->2.5mm on pi-case: extra 0.5mm for stone mortar carving depth, leaves exactly 2.0mm minimum structural wall
- Crenellations on LID not tray: preserves the lid-tray seating interface
- POST_OD 4.5->6mm: original posts were splitting on M2.5 screw insertion, now 1.95mm wall around pilot hole
- Per-type post heights: board flip means components face lid, so POST_H = CELL_H - PCB_THICK - component_height - 1mm clearance
  - Qwiic boards (4.7mm connector): 10.7mm
  - INA219 (7mm): 8.4mm
  - SSD1306 inverted (1.6mm): 13.8mm
  - Buzzer (4.7mm): 10.7mm
- Snake routing: row 0 L->R, jump at col 3, row 1 R->L — every consecutive hop is adjacent cells
- SEN0460 at end of I2C chain, TCA4307 first as bus protector
- Video/timelapse sections hide entirely when no content (no placeholder boxes)
